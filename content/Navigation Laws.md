The Navigation Acts were ==a series of English laws starting in 1651 that forced colonial trade to run through Britain on English ships to support a mercantilist economy==

- **Mercantilism:** The belief that colonies exist to enrich the mother country via raw materials and manufactured imports.

- **Key Rules:** Mandatory transport on English/colonial ships and restrictions on valuable enumerated goods.

- **Salutary Neglect:** Decades of lax enforcement permitted widespread smuggling and colonial economic autonomy.

- **Unit 2 vs. Unit 3:** Transitioned from early trade regulations to post-1763 enforcement that catalyzed colonial resentment.
