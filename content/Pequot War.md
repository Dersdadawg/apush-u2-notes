#apush/u2
1636–1638 war between English colonists (allied with the Narragansett and Mohegan) and the [[Pequot]] nation over trade, land, and a series of killings; it climaxed in the Mystic Massacre, in which English forces burned a fortified Pequot village, killing hundreds. The war effectively destroyed the Pequot as an independent power and opened Connecticut to English settlement.
