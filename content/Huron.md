#apush/u2
Confederacy of Iroquoian-speaking peoples (also called the Wendat) in the Great Lakes region who became France's key trading and military allies in [[New France]]. Their alliance with the French, dating to conflicts like Champlain's 1609 battle against the Iroquois, made them a target of the rival [[Iroquis Confederacy]], which devastated the Huron in the [[Beaver Wars]] of the 1640s.
