The New England colonies were founded by Puritans ([[Puritans]]), who sought to escape religious persecution and live as Englishmen.

The first settlement was [[Plymouth]] in 1620, which led to the [[Massachusetts Bay Colony]].

Other settlements include [[Connecticut]], [[Maine]], [[Rhode Island]], [[New Hampshire]].

In 1643, [[Massachusetts Bay Colony]], [[Plymouth]], [[New Haven]], and scattered valley settlements, joined together to form the [[New England Confederation]].

The soil was inhospitable to growing in the winter, created hardy population that was mostly English, since non-English were not incentivized to immigrate there.

Encouraged a diversified economy, did not incentivize slave trade.

They made money through shipbuilding, trading timber, and fishing. Cod was very important.
