Maryland was the second English colony founded in the Chesapeake region (after Virginia). It was founded in 1634 by [[Lord Baltimore]], a prominent Catholic. In addition to financial gain, he sought to create a haven for Catholics, who were still persecuted in England.

Colonists soon flocked to Maryland spurred on by the promise of land, but the Protestant planters from neighboring Virginia soon overthrew the haughty Catholic landlords.

But despite this Maryland succeeded through the tobacco trade. It depended also on [[Indentured Servants]].

Lord Baltimore allowed freedom of worship from the colony's founding, but as Protestant immigration swelled and Catholics became a minority, he pushed the assembly to pass the [[Act of Toleration]] in 1649 to protect Catholic settlers from persecution by the Protestant majority. It supported toleration for all who believed in Jesus Christ, but not Jews, Muslims, Hindus, etc.
