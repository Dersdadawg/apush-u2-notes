[[Roger Williams]] fled the [[Massachusetts Bay Colony]] in 1636 after being banished for his liberal religious views.

He founded the city of Providence, believing that God would provide for the colony.

He built a Baptist church, and instituted complete freedom of religion for all, including Jews and Catholics. He also sheltered the [[Quakers]], though he had many theological disagreements with them.

Rhode Island was also more democratic than its counterparts, instituting simple manhood suffrage, though later requiring property.

It eventually secured a charter from Parliament in 1644 and became a colony.
