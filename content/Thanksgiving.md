#apush/u2
Harvest celebration in autumn 1621 shared by [[Plymouth]]'s Pilgrims and the [[Wampanoag]], commemorating the colony's first successful harvest aided by [[Squanto]]. Later mythologized as a founding national story, it obscures the rapid deterioration of English-Native relations across New England in subsequent decades (see [[Puritans vs Indians]], [[King Phillip's War]]).
