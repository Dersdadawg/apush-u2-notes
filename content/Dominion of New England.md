#apush/u2 
Created by royal authority in 1686. Subsumed all territory of [[New England Confederation]], expanded to include [[New York]] and [[New Jersey]]. Also aimed at bolstering defense against the Indians.

Also intended to enforce [[Navigation Laws]].

Led by [[Sir Edmund Andros]], hated because he was Anglican, Puritans also hated his soldiers. Ran a despotic government, taxed them without representation, enforced strict Navigation Laws.

Amid the chaos of the [[Glorious Revolution]], colonists revolted in the [[Boston Revolt]].

Also led to [[Leisler's Rebellion]] in [[New York]].

Eventually charters were reissued by [[William and Mary]].
