## Representative Assembly
- [[House of Burgesses]] in [[Virginia]]
## Representative Government in New England
- [[Mayflower Compact]], early form of self-government and rudimentary written constitution.
- [[Massachusetts Bay Colony]], all members of Puritan Church could vote colony's governor and representative assembly.
## Limits to Colonial Democracy
- Many colonists were excluded because they didn't own property, women and landless men had few rights, even less for slaves and indentured servants.
- Governors ruled autocratically, such as [[Sir Edmund Andros]] or [[William Berkeley]]
