Lumbering, fishing, etc. were highly profitable

Lumbering was especially important because Britain needed the lumber to build ships

Therefore trees for lumber were marked off by the king and colonists incurred fines if they chopped them down.
This restriction on free enterprise brewed resentment among the colonists.

By 1730s, though America wanted more products from Britain because of the rapidly growing population, Britain's population plateaued and didn't need more American imports.

As a result there was a trade imbalance which led to America seeking foreign markets. This included France, including their domains in the West Indies.

However, under pressure from British West Indies planter, Parliament passed the **Molasses Act** in 1733 prohibiting trade with French West Indies, but the Americans got around by smuggling
