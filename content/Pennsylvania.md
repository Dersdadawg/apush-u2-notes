#apush/u2 
Founded by [[William Penn]], English aristocrat drawn to the faith.

Managed to secure an immense grant of fertile land from the Crown due to money owed to his father, called it "Pennsylvania".

Advertised to English, German, French, Dutch colonists, soon settled by them.

Philadelphia, city of Brotherly Love was the capital. Penn bought land from Chief Tammany, treated Indians fairly.

However, as more colonists flocked to Pennsylvania, the [[Scots-Irish]] and other groups strained relationships with Indians.
