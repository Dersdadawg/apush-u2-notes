#apush/u2 
Religious dissenters to emerge in England in mid 1600s, called that because they "quaked" when they prayed.

Officially known as the Religious Society of Friends.

Refused to pay taxes to [[Church of England]], constructed simple meetinghouses and prayed together, no ranks.

Refused military service, passively resisted.

Discriminated in both England and colonies, found homes in [[Pennsylvania]] and [[Rhode Island]].
