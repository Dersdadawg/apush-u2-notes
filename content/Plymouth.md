Some Puritans negotiated with the [[Virginia Company]] the right to settle in their territory.

They left from Plymouth, England, in the [[Mayflower]] in 1620 and landed not in [[Jamestown]] but near the coast of Massachusetts. They surveyed the lands for a while before landing in Plymouth. They were outside the jurisdiction of the Virginia Company, which made them squatters.

Before departing, they signed an important document called the [[Mayflower Compact]], an agreement for colonists to submit to the will of the majority. This set an important precedent for written documents.

The first winter of 1620-1621 left many colonists dead, but the harvest was bountiful next year, which led to the custom of [[Thanksgiving]] being celebrated.

The early colony's economy was based on fur, fish, and lumber.

The colony was governed by [[William Bradford]], an eminent scholar, who won elections over 30 times.

The colony was eventually subsumed by the much larger [[Massachusetts Bay Colony]].
