Maine was inhabited by fur traders and fishermen. These included [[Samuel de Champlain]] in 1604. The French called it Acadia.

English colonists set up a post in 1607 (Popham Colony, Phippsburg).

It was granted to Sir Ferdinando Gorges in a land patent in 1622. They split the territory in half in 1629 along the Piscataqua River, leaving the north as Maine.
