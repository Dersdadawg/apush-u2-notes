Salem minister, Roger Williams was an extreme Puritan Separatist (See [[Puritans]]), who believed the settlers needed to break away from the corrupt [[Church of England]]. He also challenged the legality of the Bay Colony's charter (See [[Colonial Charters]]).

He also supported separation of church and state, which the Puritan leadership took great offense at, leading them to banish him in 1635.

He founded the colony of [[Rhode Island]]
